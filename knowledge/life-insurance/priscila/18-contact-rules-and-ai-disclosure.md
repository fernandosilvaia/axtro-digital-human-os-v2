# Contact rules and AI disclosure (United States)

Internal guidance on the rules that shape when and how Priscila contacts someone. The consent itself is collected by the lead form and the CRM, and rules differ by state and change often, so the operator keeps the source list in the compliance folder.

## AI voices count as artificial voices

Since February 2024 the FCC treats AI generated voices as an artificial voice under the TCPA. An outbound call or voicemail made with an AI voice for telemarketing needs the person's prior express written consent, and the call must identify the caller and give an opt-out. A call that the person starts is different, but a callback they requested is an outbound call that still needs consent. Texts also count as calls for the hours rules.

## What written consent includes

A signed or e-signed agreement that names the seller, includes the number, and says clearly that signing allows autodialed or artificial voice telemarketing and is not a condition of buying anything. A 2023 FCC rule requiring consent for one seller at a time was struck down in January 2025, but the older written consent rule still applies. A lead form that names its partners can support consent only if that disclosure is really there.

## Hours and frequency

- Federal rules bar telemarketing calls and texts before 8 a.m. or after 9 p.m. at the person's location.
- Several states are stricter. Florida, Oklahoma and Maryland use 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. and a limit of three calls on the same subject in 24 hours. Washington uses 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. Texas allows 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. Monday to Saturday and noon to 9 p.m. on Sunday.
- Safe default: contact between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m. at the person's local time, noon on Sunday in Texas, and no more than three attempts in 24 hours across all numbers.

## Opt-outs and Do Not Call

- Honor "stop" in any channel, immediately. Treat a stop to a marketing call or text as a stop to all marketing from the company. The FCC adopted a rule in September 2026 that lets a company designate a clear opt-out method, and the dates are still moving.
- A company has to honor an opt-out within 10 business days at the latest, and a company do-not-call request must be honored for five years. National Do Not Call registrations last until removed.
- An inquiry from the person gives about three months of an established business relationship, which should not be treated as replacing written consent for AI voice calls.

## Telling people she is an AI

Several states require or reward clear disclosure that a person is dealing with AI, including California, Utah and Maine, and Colorado and Idaho have newer laws that take effect in 2027. Priscila says she is an AI at the start and again whenever asked, in voice, video and text. That satisfies every trigger found.

## Recording

California requires all parties to agree before a confidential call is recorded. Say that a call may be recorded or not record it.
