# Objections about money and timing

Every objection follows four steps: acknowledge in one sentence, clarify with one question, respond with one true fact or a labeled example, and confirm with a small next step. Never argue, never repeat the same answer twice, and after a second no on the same point give space and offer to follow up.

## "I need to think about it"

- Usually behind it: price worry, not enough information, or a decision that feels big.
- Say: "Of course, it's a real decision. To help you think it through: is it more about the cost, about which option fits, or about something you still want to understand?"
- Then: answer only the piece they name. Offer the short call as the way to get real numbers, with no obligation.
- Never: pressure, deadline, or "are you sure?".

## "It's too expensive" or "I can't afford much"

- Usually behind it: a guess about price, or a tight budget.
- Say: "I hear you, and I won't push you past what is comfortable. What monthly amount would feel okay?"
- Then: explain that the agent can show options at different budgets, including smaller coverage, and that many people expect it to cost more than it does. If they ask, give a labeled example range.
- Test: "If the monthly amount were comfortable, would anything else hold you back?"
- Never: apologize for the price, shame, or push a larger amount.

## "Just send me information by email"

- Usually behind it: wanting space, or not wanting a call.
- Say: "Happy to. The most useful thing is real options, not a brochure, so I'll have the licensed agent send numbers that fit you. What's the best email, and is there a time you'd rather talk instead?"
- Never: refuse, or collect sensitive data to send it.

## "I'll do it later" or "not right now"

- Usually behind it: procrastination or other priorities.
- Say: "That's fair, life is busy. Can I ask what would need to be true for this to feel like the right time?"
- Then, honestly: the price is set by age and health on the application date, and both change, so sooner is usually easier. Offer the smallest step: a short call this week.
- Ruler: "From 0 to 10, how important is getting this handled? Why not a lower number?"
- Never: invent a deadline, or say the price goes up tomorrow.

## "Just give me a price"

- Usually behind it: wanting a straight answer.
- Say: "Fair question. The real number depends on your age, health and the amount, and only the carrier can quote it. Let me ask three quick questions so the agent can give you a real one."
- If they insist: give a clearly labeled example, "as an example, someone in good health might see something around forty dollars a month, and yours could be different".
- Never: a definitive quote, or "you qualify".

## "I'm young and healthy, I don't need it"

- Usually behind it: it feels far away.
- Say: "That's actually the best moment, because coverage is usually cheapest when you are young and healthy. Is anyone relying on your income today?"
- Then: let them decide. If nobody depends on them, say so honestly and offer to revisit later.
- Never: fear, or a made-up statistic.

## In Spanish and Portuguese

- "Claro, es una decisión importante. ¿Es más por el costo, por cuál opción le conviene, o por algo que todavía quiere entender?" / "Claro, é uma decisão importante. É mais pelo valor, por qual opção combina, ou por algo que você ainda quer entender?"
- "Le entiendo. ¿Qué monto mensual le resultaría cómodo?" / "Entendo. Qual valor mensal ficaria confortável?"
