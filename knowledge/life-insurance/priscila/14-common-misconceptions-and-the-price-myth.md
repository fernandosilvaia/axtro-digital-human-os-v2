# Common misconceptions and the price myth

Use these as gentle corrections, one at a time, never as a lecture. The goal is a person who understands the product before the agent talks to them.

## The price myth

- Most adults overestimate what life insurance costs. In the 2026 LIMRA and Life Happens Insurance Barometer, about three quarters overestimated the cost, and adults under 31 overestimated a 20 year term policy by roughly five to six times.
- In the 2024 Barometer, 44 percent of Hispanic respondents said life insurance is too expensive and 72 percent overestimated the cost of term life.
- Cost is also the top reason people who feel underinsured give for not buying. So when cost comes up, offer a clearly labeled example range and ask what monthly amount would feel comfortable. The real price comes from the carrier quote.

## Misconceptions to correct gently

- "A 0 percent floor means my money is protected from any loss." Index credits cannot go below the floor, but policy charges are deducted every month, so the cash value can still fall.
- "Dividends are guaranteed because the company always paid them." Dividends are never guaranteed.
- "Guaranteed universal life is guaranteed no matter what I pay." The guarantee depends on paying at least the required premium on time.
- "Tax free means cost free." Surrender charges, new acquisition costs and a new contestability period can still apply to an exchange.
- "Living benefits are extra money." They are paid out of the death benefit, so the family receives less later.
- "A fixed annuity is basically a bank account that pays more." It is an insurance contract, not a bank deposit, not FDIC insured, and early withdrawals can cost surrender charges and taxes.
- "A graded benefit policy pays nothing at first." In the first two years a natural death usually returns the premiums paid plus a set percentage or interest, and an accidental death usually pays in full.
- "The funeral cost figures include the cemetery." Typical published medians exclude cemetery, monument and cash advance items.
- "Social Security or the government will cover it." The Social Security death payment is a one-time 255 dollars for a spouse or some minor children.
- "Mortgage protection is a special product that always pays my mortgage." The label covers different products. Ask who the beneficiary is.
- "I have insurance at work, so I am covered." Group coverage often ends with the job, though many plans allow a short window to convert or port it. In the 2026 Barometer, about a quarter of people with life insurance had only workplace coverage, and some felt it was not enough.
- "An instant decision means I am approved." Some carriers decide in minutes using data, but if the data is not enough the application moves to the usual underwriting steps.
- "New illustration rules mean the policy credits less." The rules limit what a sales illustration may show. They do not change what a policy credits.

## Spanish and Portuguese notes

Say "ejemplo" or "exemplo" every time a price is mentioned. In Spanish use "usted" unless the person invites "tú". In Portuguese, "prêmio" is the insurance premium and is easy to confuse with "prize", so say "valor mensal do seguro" when talking to someone new to the term.
