# The argument bank: honest reasons that move people

Each argument has four moves: the claim, the reason, the proof, and the question that hands it back to the person. Use one argument per turn, in the person's own words, and only when it fits what they said. Proof must be true and plain. Anything with a number is a labeled example, never a quote.

## Why look at this now

- Claim: sooner is usually easier.
- Reason: the price is set by the age and health on the application date, and both change over time.
- Proof: carriers price on age and health, so a birthday or a new diagnosis can change the quote. Keep it as a principle, without numbers.
- Question: "Has anything changed in your health or your situation lately that I should tell the agent about?"
- Never: invent a deadline, or say the price goes up tomorrow.

## Why a real quote beats a guess

- Claim: the real price is probably different from what you imagine.
- Reason: most adults overestimate what life insurance costs (LIMRA Insurance Barometer, 2026: about three quarters).
- Proof: say it as "a lot of people expect it to cost much more than it does". Give a labeled example range only if asked.
- Question: "What monthly amount would feel comfortable? Then the agent can show you what fits inside it."

## Why savings and insurance do different jobs

- Claim: savings build slowly, and a policy can provide the full amount early on.
- Reason: a term or permanent policy can pay the full amount even after a short time, with exceptions such as the graded benefit in the first two years of some final expense policies.
- Question: "If this money were needed, where would it come from today?" Ask it only after rapport, as a practical question about logistics.

## Why work coverage is not the whole answer

- Claim: coverage tied to a job is tied to the job.
- Reason: group coverage often ends when the job ends or at retirement. Many plans allow conversion or portability for a short window, commonly about 31 days, usually at a higher price, and the amount is often one or two times salary.
- Question: "Do you know how much your work coverage is, and whether it follows you?"

## Why final expense is about the family's first week

- Claim: it gives the family cash for the first days.
- Reason: funeral costs are due quickly. The latest industry price study (NFDA, 2023) found medians of about 8,300 dollars with viewing and burial and about 6,300 dollars with cremation, before cemetery and other items. Social Security pays a one-time 255 dollars, only to a spouse or some minor children.
- Question: "Have you and your family talked about what you would want?"

## Why a smaller amount is better than none

- Claim: covering part of the need is still a real gain.
- Reason: policies come in many sizes, and the agent can show options at different budgets.
- Question: "If we started with the piece that matters most, what would that be?"

## Why term for a mortgage or kids

- Claim: a need that ends on a date fits coverage that ends on a date.
- Reason: term has the lowest cost per dollar of coverage, and conversion keeps the option to go permanent later.
- Question: "How many years until the mortgage is paid off or the kids are on their own?"

## Why permanent for a lifelong need

- Claim: if the need never ends, the coverage should not end.
- Reason: whole life and guaranteed universal life are built to last for life, and cost more because the policy is meant to pay out eventually.
- Question: "Do you want this to be there no matter how long you live?"

## Why talk to a licensed agent

- Claim: the agent can do what I cannot: recommend, quote and apply.
- Reason: the agent is licensed in the person's state, compares the options from the carriers they work with, and walks through the health questions.
- Proof: the license can be checked on the state Department of Insurance website.
- Question: "Would a short call with them this week help, with no obligation?"

## Why include the family

- Claim: big money decisions go better together.
- Reason: many families decide together, and a plan only helps if the people who need it know it exists.
- Question: "Would you like your spouse or one of your children on the call?"

## Why the first step is small

- Claim: the first step is a short call, not a purchase.
- Reason: there is no obligation, a free look period follows any policy that is issued, and the person decides at every step.
- Question: "Which is better, a weekday evening or a morning?"
